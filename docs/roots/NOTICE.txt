Morpheme data — CC BY-SA 4.0
----------------------------
Roots, prefixes, suffixes, example words, breakdowns and etymology notes are
adapted from jupsh/anki-word-roots, commit d5f7bd1ab23a29832e4b7ee8de558177229b5e2e.
That data is CC BY-SA 4.0 because it comes from Wikipedia:

- List of Greek and Latin roots in English, by Wikipedia contributors
- List of medical roots, suffixes and prefixes, by Wikipedia contributors

anki-word-roots reviewed the lists, kept common example words, and added
decode breakdowns and sourced stories. Upstream statement:
https://github.com/jupsh/anki-word-roots
License deed: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Chinese wording for a root, affix, breakdown gloss, literal sense or story is
a translation of that English source string. Those translations are part of
the same CC BY-SA 4.0 adaptation. They are not new definitions.

You may share and adapt this morpheme material, including commercially, if you
give credit and license the adaptation under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Chinese dictionary lines — MIT
------------------------------
Short Chinese senses, pronunciations and exam tags on individual words come
from ECDICT, https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT , commit
39cfb9672c0906b63569e0453c2e6abfb9ab182a.
Copyright (c) 2025 Linwei. MIT License.
The license text is tools/roots/vendor/ECDICT-LICENSE.txt.

Only the senses attached to words on this site are included. A word with no
ECDICT line is shown without a Chinese definition. Exam lists are the ECDICT
rows tagged cet4, cet6, ielts, toefl, gre or zk (中考).
