BOKO: Space Escape 隐私政策

BOKO: Space Escape Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-28

1. 概述 / Overview

BOKO: Space Escape 是一款离线街机生存游戏。我们重视你的隐私，只在设备上处理运行游戏所需的信息。

BOKO: Space Escape is an offline arcade survival game. We respect your privacy and process only the information needed to run the game on your device.

2. 我们收集的信息 / Information We Collect

本 App 不要求注册，不含广告、第三方分析或追踪 SDK。开发者不会通过本 App 收集或接收你的姓名、邮箱、电话、位置、设备标识符或游戏使用记录，也没有开发者服务器。

The app does not require registration and contains no advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs. The developer does not collect or receive your name, email, phone number, location, device identifiers, or gameplay activity through the app. There is no developer-operated server.

3. 设备本地数据 / Data Stored on Your Device

本 App 使用 Apple 的 UserDefaults 在设备本地保存游戏设置、最高分和部分累计进度。这些数据不会上传到开发者服务器。删除 App 会移除随 App 保存的本地数据。

The app uses Apple's UserDefaults to store game settings, your best score, and some cumulative progress locally on your device. This information is not uploaded to a developer server. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data.

4. Apple Game Center / Apple Game Center

你可以选择登录 Apple Game Center，以使用排行榜和成就。启用这些功能后，分数和成就进度会通过 Apple Game Center 提交并由 Apple 按其隐私条款处理；开发者不会收到 Game Center 账号凭证。Game Center 为可选功能，核心游戏支持离线游玩。

You may optionally sign in to Apple Game Center to use leaderboards and achievements. When enabled, scores and achievement progress are submitted through Apple Game Center and handled by Apple under its privacy terms. The developer does not receive Game Center credentials. Game Center is optional; core gameplay works offline.

5. 数据共享与儿童隐私 / Sharing and Children's Privacy

开发者不会出售或共享通过本 App 收集的个人数据，也不会有意收集儿童个人信息。

The developer does not sell or share personal data collected through the app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. 联系方式 / Contact

如有隐私问题，请通过 yisiliang@foxmail.com 联系开发者。

For privacy questions, contact the developer at yisiliang@foxmail.com.

7. 政策更新 / Changes

如本 App 的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面及日期。

If the app's data practices change, this page and its date will be updated.
