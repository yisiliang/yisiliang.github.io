基本操作 / Getting Started

  1. 在主菜单点击“开始逃亡” / Start Run。
  2. 拖动屏幕左下角的虚拟摇杆移动波克。
  3. 点击右下角技能按钮，在敌群包围时施放技能。
  4. 击败追兵并拾取废料以提高分数。
  1. Tap “Start Run” on the main menu.
  2. Drag the virtual joystick at the bottom left to move BOKO.
  3. Tap a skill button at the bottom right when enemies close in.
  4. Defeat pursuers and collect salvage to build your score.

常见问题 / Troubleshooting

  • 移动无响应：在左下角区域按住并拖动；点击不会移动角色。
  • 技能不可用：先拾取对应充能，再点击技能按钮。
  • 排行榜或成就不可用：登录 Game Center 并检查网络连接；核心玩法无需联网。
  • 游戏暂停：返回 App 后点击继续即可恢复。
  • No movement: press and drag in the bottom-left area; tapping alone does not move BOKO.
  • Skills unavailable: collect the matching charge before tapping its skill button.
  • Leaderboard or achievements unavailable: sign in to Game Center and check your network. Core gameplay works offline.
  • Game paused: return to the app and tap Continue to resume.
