轻秤同步隐私政策

Scale2Health Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-31

1. 概述

轻秤同步是一款iPhone App，通过蓝牙接收体重秤的测量数据，并在你确认后写入Apple健康。App本地优先运行，数据处理仅限实现这些功能所必需的范围。

Scale2Health is an iPhone app that receives weight measurements from a Bluetooth scale and, after your confirmation, writes them to Apple Health. It works locally and limits data processing to what these features require.

2. 我们收集哪些信息

轻秤同步无需注册账号，没有后端服务器，不包含广告、第三方分析或用户跟踪SDK，不会把体重、健康数据、设备标识符或位置信息上传给开发者或任何第三方。

Scale2Health requires no account, has no backend server, and includes no advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs. It does not upload weight, health data, device identifiers, or location to the developer or any third party.

3. 本地存储内容

  • 从体重秤接收的测量记录、已选体重秤的蓝牙标识及App设置保存在设备本地，使用iOS文件保护，并排除在备份之外。
  • 蓝牙诊断报文只保留在内存中；只有在你主动点击“复制诊断”时才会复制到剪贴板，默认不含原始报文。
  • 你可以在App内清除历史；删除App会删除全部App本地数据。

Measurements received from the scale, the selected scale's Bluetooth identifier, and app settings are stored locally with iOS file protection and excluded from backups. Diagnostic packets stay in memory only. Deleting the app removes all of its local data.

4. Apple健康（HealthKit）

  • 轻秤同步只请求写入“体重”的权限，不请求任何健康数据读取权限，不会查看或修改其他App的健康记录。
  • 体重只有在你勾选并确认是本人的记录后才会写入健康；自动同步默认关闭，需你自行开启。
  • 通过HealthKit获得的数据不会用于广告、营销或数据挖掘，也不会出售或共享给第三方。

Scale2Health only requests permission to write Body Mass and never reads health data. Weights are written only after you confirm they are yours; automatic sync is off by default. HealthKit data is never used for advertising, marketing, or data mining, and is never sold or shared.

5. 蓝牙与系统权限

蓝牙权限仅用于发现和连接你的体重秤并接收测量数据。拒绝蓝牙权限将无法接收体重；拒绝健康权限时，记录仍保留在App本地。

Bluetooth permission is used only to find and connect to your scale and receive measurements. Without Health permission, records remain in the app locally.

6. 数据共享与儿童隐私

轻秤同步不会出售、出租或共享用户数据，也不会有意收集儿童个人信息。

Scale2Health does not sell, rent, or share user data and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. 联系方式

如有隐私相关问题，请通过yisiliang@foxmail.com联系开发者。

For privacy-related questions, contact the developer at yisiliang@foxmail.com.

8. 政策更新

如果轻秤同步的数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面和生效日期。

If Scale2Health's data practices change, this page and its effective date will be updated.
