PhotoTimeFix 隐私政策

PhotoTimeFix Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-23

1. 概述

PhotoTimeFix 是一个用于根据照片和视频文件名解析时间，并在用户确认后修正系统相册创建时间的工具。我们重视你的隐私，并将所有处理限制在设备本地完成。

PhotoTimeFix is a utility for parsing dates from photo and video file names and, after user confirmation, fixing creation dates in the system photo library. We respect your privacy and keep all processing local on your device.

2. 我们收集哪些信息

PhotoTimeFix 不会创建账号，也不会主动收集以下信息：

  • 姓名、邮箱、电话等身份信息
  • 位置信息
  • 联系人
  • 使用分析数据
  • 广告标识符或追踪数据

PhotoTimeFix does not create user accounts and does not intentionally collect the following information:

  • Name, email address, phone number, or other identity information
  • Location information
  • Contacts
  • Analytics or usage-tracking data
  • Advertising identifiers or tracking data

3. App 如何处理照片和视频

在你授予照片库权限后，PhotoTimeFix 会在设备本地访问系统照片库，用于：

  • 读取照片和视频的缩略图
  • 读取媒体文件的原始文件名
  • 读取当前创建时间
  • 根据支持的文件名规则解析新时间
  • 在你确认后批量修改选中媒体的创建时间

这些操作都在你的设备本地完成。PhotoTimeFix 不会将照片、视频、文件名或时间信息上传到开发者服务器。

After you grant photo library access, PhotoTimeFix accesses the system photo library locally on your device in order to:

  • Read thumbnails for photos and videos
  • Read original media file names
  • Read current creation dates
  • Parse new dates from supported file name formats
  • Modify creation dates for selected media after your confirmation

These operations are performed locally on your device. PhotoTimeFix does not upload photos, videos, file names, or timestamp information to the developer's servers.

4. 本地存储内容

PhotoTimeFix 可能会在 App 运行期间临时保存界面状态，例如扫描进度、已识别项目和勾选状态。这些内容仅用于当前本地功能，不会被出售或共享给第三方。

PhotoTimeFix may temporarily store interface state while the app is running, such as scan progress, recognized items, and selected items. This information is used only for local app functionality and is not sold or shared with third parties.

5. 第三方服务

截至本隐私政策更新日期，PhotoTimeFix 未集成第三方广告、统计或用户跟踪 SDK。

As of the last updated date of this policy, PhotoTimeFix does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or user-tracking SDKs.

6. 数据共享

PhotoTimeFix 不会将你的照片、视频、文件名、创建时间或本地处理结果出售、出租或共享给第三方。

PhotoTimeFix does not sell, rent, or share your photos, videos, file names, creation dates, or local processing results with third parties.

7. 儿童隐私

PhotoTimeFix 不面向儿童提供社交、广告或用户生成内容发布功能，也不会有意收集儿童个人信息。

PhotoTimeFix does not provide social, advertising, or user-generated content publishing features for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. 你的控制权

  • 你可以在 iOS 设置中撤销照片库权限。
  • 你可以不勾选任何媒体，避免执行时间修正。
  • 你可以删除 App，从设备上移除 App 本身及其本地状态。

You can, at any time:

  • Revoke photo library access in iOS Settings.
  • Avoid selecting any media so no date changes are performed.
  • Delete the app to remove the app and its local state from your device.

9. 联系方式

如有隐私相关问题，请通过 yisiliang@foxmail.com 联系开发者。

If you have privacy-related questions, please contact the developer at yisiliang@foxmail.com.

10. 政策更新

如果 PhotoTimeFix 的隐私实践发生变化，我们会在发布新版本时更新本隐私政策。

If PhotoTimeFix's privacy practices change, this privacy policy will be updated in a future app release.
